# Reviewed publication boundary

New proposals require a human reviewer, evidence note, verified review status, current review date, HTTPS source, provider confirmation requirement, participationType (contribution, learning, social), geographicRelevance (charleston-sc), eligibility (adult_public), and kind (recurring_program or scheduled_event). Scheduled events require a future startsAt and deadline no later than start. The CLI proposes a complete inventory; it does not authenticate reviewers or publish automatically. Reviewers must establish these attributes from official sources. Existing inventory is legacy and must be re-reviewed before pilot activation. The pilot remains off. Do not infer member geography or age from behavior; explicit adult consent and Charleston scope are separate activation gates.

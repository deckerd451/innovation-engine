# Charleston provider evidence, November 26, 2026

The linked official pages were independently opened and inspected for role, location and a public participation route. The 26 publishable rows in [the reviewed inventory](../data/hpe-release-reviewed-evidence.json) cover seven providers. Roles sharing an intake page have distinct duties and stable pathway IDs. A row represents an actionable application, registration or inquiry. Providers confirm eligibility, screening, placement and open slots.

| Provider | Pathways | How to start |
|---|---:|---|
| [Charleston Animal Society](https://charlestonanimalsociety.org/volunteer/) | 6 | Apply at the Charleston campus and book orientation. The main program is 18+ and some roles require further training. |
| [Lowcountry Food Bank](https://lowcountryfoodbank.org/get-involved/volunteer/) | 1 | Register in Golden for Charleston warehouse volunteering. The provider requires closed-toe shoes. |
| [One80 Place](https://www.one80place.org/volunteer) | 7 | Use an individual role's registration link and complete the waiver. Volunteers must be 18+. |
| [Charleston Habitat for Humanity](https://www.charlestonhabitat.org/get-involved/volunteer/) | 4 | Use the calendar for construction or ReStore, the office contact for administration, or the interest form for CORE training. |
| [Charleston FRIENDS of the Library](https://www.charlestonlibraryfriends.org/volunteer) | 4 | Use the provider's Volunteer Interest Survey and await follow-up. |
| [The Charleston Museum](https://www.charlestonmuseum.org/volunteers/) | 3 | Submit the provider's interest and availability form and await staff follow-up. |
| [Lowcountry Orphan Relief](https://www.lowcountryorphanrelief.org/volunteer/volunteer-with-us) | 1 | Apply for the North Charleston distribution center, where sorting and care kit packing are assigned together. |

This ledger supersedes the earlier evidence and candidate files. City Recreation and Parks Conservancy remain discovery candidates outside this release inventory. Earlier dates and role counts did not carry forward. No signup was submitted and individual places were not checked.
